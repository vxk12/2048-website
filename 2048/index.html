What is stored

Downloading from the App Store

The app is distributed exclusively through Apple's App Store. When you download, install or update it, Apple processes data such as your Apple Account, device and purchase information as an independent controller under its own privacy policy. This happens entirely on Apple's side and is outside our control.

From Apple we receive aggregated sales and download figures. In addition, crash reports and usage statistics reach us — but only from people who have turned on "Share With App Developers" under Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements. Apple aggregates this data using privacy-preserving techniques so that it does not identify anyone personally.

One exception: if you publish a rating or a review, we can see the nickname and text you chose and may reply to it publicly. The nickname shields your identity — only Apple knows who is behind it. This all takes place on Apple's platform under Apple's terms, not on ours.

Your rights

If you have questions about privacy, write directly to rttg.dev@gmail.com — the same address listed in the Imprint below. Since no personal data is stored on any server, requests regarding stored personal data are generally not applicable for the current version of the app.
